# Voice samples

Generated with [eSpeak NG](https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng) (voice `en-us+m3`, pitch 28, 150 wpm) by `tools/make-voice.mjs`.
They are the raw material for the vocal chops in the track; they are pitched, crushed and effected in `src/audio/voices.mjs`.
The texts are the three title words of the klais-16 README (SIXTEEN / SEGMENT / DISPLAY), the product name and their syllables.
